In the valley

Mường Thanh valley tours

Mường Thanh is the largest rice basin in Vietnam's northwest and the site of the 1954 battle that ended French rule in Indochina — a working farm valley with a war history folded into it. These trips stay inside the bowl of hills around Điện Biên Phủ.

Over the border

Overland into Laos

Practical

The Tây Trang crossing, waypoint by waypoint

Land crossings reward planning more than any other part of a Laos trip. Visa rules at Tây Trang have changed more than once in the last few years — currently e-visa, visa-on-arrival, and embassy-issued Vietnamese visas can all exit here, but Lao visa-on-arrival is not issued at this post, so Lao entry needs to be arranged beforehand. We keep this current and handle it for anyone booking a Laos trip with us.

Ask about visas for this trip
  1. 0 km

    Điện Biên Phủ

    Starting point — hotels, the bus station, and currency exchange around the central market.

  2. 35 km

    Tây Trang border post (VN)

    Vietnamese exit checkpoint. Passport control and customs; no visa-on-arrival issued here.

  3. ~40 km

    Pang Hok checkpoint (LA)

    Lao entry post, also known as Sop Hun. Pre-arranged Lao entry clears fastest here.

  4. ~140 km

    Muang Khua, Laos

    Riverside town on the Nam Ou — the usual overnight stop before continuing south.

  5. ~250 km

    Luang Prabang

    By road, or by slow boat down the Nam Ou from Muang Khua in the dry season.
